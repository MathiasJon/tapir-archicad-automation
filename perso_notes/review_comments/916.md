Maintainer review: DevKit and live checks on Archicad 29.

- **DevKit:** in AC25/26, `APIEnv_ChangeCurrLayerCombID` takes `par1: API_AttributeIndex*` (APIdefs_Environment.h). AC27 and AC29 declare `ACAPI_Navigator_ChangeCurrLayerComb (API_AttributeIndex*)`. The shim and the call are right.
- **Live:** activating a combination that hides a layer hides it in the current window, and activating one that shows it makes it visible again. An unknown guid is rejected.
- **Blocking: the guid of a *Layer* is accepted.** `ACAPI_Attribute_Get` by guid also finds attributes of other types, so the Layer's index is then used as a layer combination index. The command returns `success` and activates an unrelated combination. Reading the combination back by index and comparing guids fixes it:
